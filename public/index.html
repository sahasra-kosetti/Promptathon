Distributed Replication Mechanics

1
Split Buffer into 2 Equal Chunks

Uploaded object is partitioned into chunk_0 and chunk_1 with midpoint boundary calculation.

2
Cryptographic SHA-256 Fingerprinting

Every chunk receives an immutable cryptographic digest for guaranteed bit-rot detection during reads.

3
Simultaneous Active-Node Replication

Replicas are written to all currently online virtual drives (./storage/node_X).

4
Fault-Tolerant Auto-Failover Reassembly

If any drive is crashed or bit-rotted, surviving replicas are automatically probed and stitched together seamlessly.
